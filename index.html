Delivering secure and resilient networks since 2014

Established in 2014 in Bangalore, Solvent Network Solutions began as a network product sales organization and grew into a full-service IT systems integrator. Today we undertake complex structured network projects for voice, data and video applications across enterprises and SMBs.

We focus on UTMs, cybersecurity, VPN, Hotspot, SD-WAN, backup solutions, endpoint security, firewalls, servers, AD/DNS, LAN/WAN, wireless and IP CCTV.
